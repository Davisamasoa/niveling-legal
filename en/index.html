Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026Brazilian law 13.709/2018 (LGPD)

Ascend is a workout app with game elements (level, XP, ranks, achievements). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, where it is kept and how you can delete it. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018), the law that applies to the app.

Controller of the app and the data: Davi Machado, Ascend's developer. Contact: davisamasoa@gmail.com.

02Device permissions

  • Notifications: workout and daily quest reminders. They are scheduled on the device itself; nothing is sent to servers.
  • Photos (save to gallery): used only when you ask to save an image (weekly report, card for stories). The app writes the image to the gallery; it does not read or upload your photos.

03Who the data is shared with

Only with the services that make the app work, which process the data on our behalf and only for that:

  • Supabase: accounts and progress backup.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google, to confirm your identity.
  • Sentry: error reports.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: only if you subscribe to Ascend Premium — payment and subscription status.
  • Groq (AI provider): only if you generate an AI plan — it receives only the training preferences described above.

If you join a league, your player name, avatar, rank, title, subscriber badge and league XP are shown to the other players in it. No other data of yours is shown to other players.

These services may store data on servers outside Brazil. All communication between the app and them is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not sell personal data.

04How long we keep it

  • Account and backup: for as long as your account exists. When you delete the account, your email and backup are deleted from our servers.
  • Data on the device: until you delete your progress in the app or uninstall it.
  • Error reports: deleted automatically by Sentry after the service's retention period (90 days at most).

05How to delete your account and data

IN THE APPSettings → Cloud backup → Delete account. The account and the cloud backup are deleted immediately and permanently.

WITHOUT THE APPFor example, if you already uninstalled it: email davisamasoa@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Ascend account”, from the email registered in the account. We delete the account and the backup within 30 days and confirm by email.

To delete only the progress on the device, without deleting the account: Settings → Delete progress.

06Your rights (LGPD)

You can ask at any time for: confirmation that we process your data, access to it, correction, a copy (portability) and deletion. The app already lets you export your progress to a file (Settings → File backup) and delete your account. For any other request, write to the contact above.

07Children

Ascend is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you know of an account belonging to a child, write to the contact above and it will be deleted.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and, if the change is significant, we will let you know in the app. The Portuguese version is the original; if the translations differ, the Portuguese version prevails.
